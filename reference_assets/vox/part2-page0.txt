Wireframe


The redesign keeps the existing VOX visual language. The change is interaction-focused, not a complete visual
redesign. Wireframes validate the flow and the venue-switching logic; final screens apply the VOX visual language to
the validated flow





Prototype Screens


The redesign connects movie discovery, cinema selection, showtime comparison, seat selection, food ordering, and
payment into a consistent booking journey. Its central UX objective is to make cinema switching easier when seat
availability  is  limited, while preserving  relevant booking context and  clearly communicating any changes  to,
showtimes, formats, or pricing.

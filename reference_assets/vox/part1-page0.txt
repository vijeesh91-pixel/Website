     Vox Cinema Booking experience: Reducing
       Friction When Switching Cinemas





           At-A-Glance


             A self-initiated redesign project exploring how the VOX Cinemas booking experience could be made simpler, clearer,
               and more intuitive. The project focuses on identifying usability gaps in the existing experience and proposing a more
                  streamlined movie-booking journey.

          Problem Statement


               The current VOX booking experience follows a linear, venue-specific journey, making  it difficult for users to change
                cinemas once they have started booking. When users reach the seat-selection stage and find limited availability, there
                        is no clear way to explore or compare another venue without leaving the flow.

                  This creates a loss-of-context problem: users may have to repeat earlier steps, lose their booking progress, and
                  mentally track information from the previous venue. For a time-sensitive, high-intent task like cinema booking, this
               adds unnecessary cognitive load and increases the risk of flow abandonment.

            How might we let users compare and switch venues at any point before payment, without losing their progress or
                confidence in the booking?



            Constraints and Assumptions

               The redesign was approached within the boundaries of VOX Cinemasˇ existing booking ecosystem. The goal was to
                improve the venue-selection and switching experience without changing the underlying booking, inventory, pricing, or
               payment systems.

              Intuitive and Heuristics Analysis


             VOX does show the selected venue throughout the booking journey   but it exposes the venue as information, not as
                an actionable booking control.


            The user's mental model

                People think in terms of "this movie, around this time, somewhere convenient." Cinema is a flexible detail, and the
                movie and time are the commitments. The booking flow assumes the opposite, treating venue as fixed once a
               showtime is clicked.


                     Current Mental model((

                  Choose cinema ™ Choose showtime ™ Enter booking ™ Choose seats ™ Discover poor availability





                     Desired Mental model((

                  Choose cinema ™ Choose showtime ™ Enter booking ™ Choose seats ™ Discover poor availability ™
                   Check other cinemas ™ Choose another cinema ™ Choose showtime ™ Select Seat ™ Continue

              Design Opportunity

             VOX maintains venue visibility throughout the booking journey, but does not provide an equivalent venue-editing
               mechanism when the user discovers new information at seat selection.When seat availability is poor, let users check
                  other cinemas without leaving the booking context, then revalidate only the information that depends on the selected
                 venue.
